# EchoFin — AI Financial Call Intelligence

EchoFin is an AI-powered system that transforms financial customer service calls into actionable intelligence. It analyzes call audio to understand *why* a customer called, *what* was discussed, and *what actions* should follow.

---

## What EchoFin Does

1. **Ingests call recordings**
2. **Converts speech to text (ASR)**
3. **Identifies primary customer intent**
   - Payment commitment
   - Payment delay
   - Inquiry
   - Complaint
4. **Extracts key financial context**
   - Monetary amounts
   - Dates & timelines
   - Commitments
5. **Analyzes customer sentiment**
6. **Builds a continuous customer intelligence profile**

Each processed call enriches the customer’s interaction history, enabling smarter decisions over time.
